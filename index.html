02 · Origin

The farm is not a label. It is the entire reason the cup tastes the way it does.

We fly to Yirgacheffe twice a year. To Aceh once. To Toraja every harvest. We sit with the farmers, we cup the lots on-site, and we pay above the C-market price — every time, without negotiation.

The result is eight to eleven single-origin lots per year. No blends. No mystery. Just the bean, the soil it grew in, and the people who picked it.

03 · Process

Eighteen minutes. Three stages. One roaster.

The Probat L12 runs on gas, not electricity, which means we can hold a steady drum temperature for the full duration. Every batch passes through the same three stages.

01 / DRY PHASE

Evaporating the moisture

Drum at 198°C. Green beans lose 12% of their mass as water vapor. Smells like hay, then toast, then — finally — coffee.

Duration 07:30
02 / MAILLARD

The browning stage

Sugars and amino acids begin to bind. Color shifts from straw to amber. We listen for the first crack at 196°C — the signal that the bean has become coffee.

Duration 06:00
03 / DEVELOP

Where the roast lives or dies

Four and a half minutes between first crack and drop. Too short, the cup is grassy. Too long, it is flat. We drop at 212°C — every time.

Duration 04:30
